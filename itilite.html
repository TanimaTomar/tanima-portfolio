Employee Travel Tracker [2020 - 6 months]

Helping companies locate and support traveling employees during COVID-19

Itilite is a business travel management platform. I joined during the early months of COVID, when travel was restricted but business-critical trips still had to happen. Companies needed a way to know where their traveling employees actually were — both to stay compliant and to reach the nearest available person in an emergency.

My role

I designed the UX and UI for a real-time tracker showing employees' current travel status and location — flights in progress, hotel stays, and trip details — so organizations could see who was traveling, where, and reach out quickly if needed.

List view (top) for detailed trip records, map view (bottom) for an at-a-glance geographic picture — 80 traveling, 69 staying, tracked in real time.

Outcome

This stayed in the design phase through my time at Itilite — it hadn't moved to development yet when I left.
