[This time]

I didn't want to write another About section that tells you I'm hardworking, curious, and collaborative. I've used those words for years to describe my work. This time, I wanted to tell you about the things that existed long before the résumé did.

Curiosity

As a child, my father and I had very important questions to solve.
Could a balloon make a boat run?
Could we make a parachute for my doll?
I think that's where it began.
I've learned that curiosity doesn't require extraordinary circumstances. Sometimes, it simply requires being open enough to notice the ordinary and ask: What else could this be?

Empathy

I have come to think of empathy less as understanding someone and more as temporarily stepping outside your own reality. I have spent years designing for people I may never meet. Now, as I move toward designing experiences for children, that ability feels even more important.
How do you create a world for someone whose world is not your own?
Maybe it begins by being willing to leave your own for a while.

Wonder

My father rarely got summer holidays.
So my brother and I invented ours. One day, we were dacoits and police. The next, our home was a car-racing arena. Nothing around us had changed. We had simply changed what it could be.
Maybe that's what wonder means to me - finding possibility in what is already there.

And perhaps that's why I keep finding my way back to children.
Maybe that's what I'm still drawn to - that little moment when something ordinary suddenly becomes possible.
